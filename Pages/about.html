Who We Are

Welcome! If you're wondering who or what we are, you've come to the right place! We are a collection of identities sharing one body named the Rainbow Parade. This phenomenon is called plurality, and each person who is plural can refer to themselves as a group. We use the term system to refer to ourselves. We are a large system with many active and dormant members. Our origins are possibly traumagenic or mixed, and we believe that plurality comes in many forms.


It's been many years since we found out that we were plural. We'd say that we're far enough along on our healing journey to process what happened to us.


This site is intended to be a space where we can express ourselves fully. A digital place to call home, we suppose.
