Frequently Asked Questions






Why Rainbow Parade?

We ended up landing on this name because we're a group of headmates that vary differently from each other. Each one of us are vibrant and unique, just like the colors of the world. We even have our own favorite colors that differ from each other.


We also have a system mascot! A little creature named Rainbow and they represent us all.




What type of system are ya'll?

We're kinda sure that we're traumagenic in nature, though many of us have seperate memories from before the trauma. In that case, maybe we're a mixed-origin system? We're not quite sure what we are regardless of whether that's true. Either way, the past trauma [and sometimes recent trauma] impacts us in a very big way, including how we function.




What does your headspace look like?

We exist on different "layers" or "realms" from each other. There's a lot of them, and each layer has headmates that live there. We have many layers, too many to list here. Primarily, the main fronters are from four areas: The Underground, the Border, the Forest, and the Dreamscape. Each one of them have a fully realized world/space to them. They are also considered dangerous areas to enter unless a headmate is prepared or lives in said environment.


The Underground is a post-apocalyptic magical world. Jay and Sammy originate from there.


The Border is an extension of the underground, abandoned buildings litter the grey landscape. Nurse is from here.


The Dreamscape is a space-like layer found below the border. It's where all of our dreams play out when we sleep. Greenstar, Bluestar, and Nathan are from here.


The Forest is a magical place where many memories are contained. Gabe lives here.


When we want to front or be social, there is an apartment lobby that we go to. The fronter sits at the computer, and any co-fronters watch them interact.




Where is [Headmate]'s page? Why are they not listed in the system list?

Some of us are shy, or don't want to publically be known by others. Some of them feel as if they need a full page to describe themselves, or they want it to be an encounter through the Rainbow Zone. If you want a comprehensive list of all headmates that have a page, please see our site map.




Can we ask more questions than what's listed here?

Feel free to! We like talking about our plurality to folks.
