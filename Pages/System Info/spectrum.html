Memory Sharing: How many memories and time do we share together? The more bars, the more that memories and experieces are shared.


  • We share some memories, but most of the time memories pertain to the headmate that experienced them.



    • Co-operation: How well do your headmates work together as a team? The more bars, the better the teamwork.


  • We'd say that most of the fronters and co-conscient folks are pretty good at working together.



    • System Stability: How stable are we as a system? The higher the bars, the less clinical distress we face as a system.


  • Sometimes we struggle with our traumas, but we've worked out the majority of our clinical distress. Mind you, it didn't used to be so stress-free. We've come a long way.



    • Internal Communication: How well we communiate between headmates. The higher the bars, the better we communicate with each other.


  • Our ability to communicate with each other is fairly high. We can't reach everyone, but we can convey information between the headmates we do know well.



    • Ability to Switch: How smooth is it for your system to switch? The more bars there are, the easier it is to switch.


  • We switch easily with little issues. It's mostly because we needed to react to dangerous situations in a timely manner. Now, it's stuck that way.



    • Co-consciousness: How easily we share time and awareness together. The more consciousness we share, the higher the bars.


  • We are able to share time and awareness if there is more than one headmate around at a time.



    • Elaboration: How much individuality do our headmates have? Do they have their own name, age, gender, hobbies, relationships and beliefs? The more distinct they are from each other, the higher the bars.


  • We are extremely individualistic. Our system name celebrates how differnt we are from each other. We take pride in it.



    • Overt: How open are we as a system to the outside world? The more bars there are, the more open we are.


  • We are currently only open online and in certain public spaces, like conventions. We aren't really open publically or at work, as we believe that would cause issues down the line.



    • Pride: How much pride do you have for being a system?


  • We are extremely prideful about who we are. Not only have we survived so much to get here, we also appreciate our plurality. There's nothing wrong with who we are, and we think that's beautiful.



    • Systemfluidity: How does our gender relate to our plurality?


  • We have a lot of different genders in our system. It's currently a three way split between masculine, feminine, and non-binary/agender folks. As a result, there is a lot of gender dysphoria and euphoria at the same time for us.
